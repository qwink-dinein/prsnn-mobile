Decisions for you before I build: (1) Commission: the PRD says 25% (companion gets 75%: ₹974 on ₹1,299); the app pays 80% today. These screens show 75%. (2) Taxes and platform fee: shown as “Platform fee ₹0 · Taxes included”. Please confirm GST treatment with your CA before launch. (3) Categories: the PRD’s five replace the eight from the last round; launch with Don’t Go Alone, New to the City, Explore Together (+ Walk & Study Buddy), Loved Ones as a separate pilot. (4) Booking order: category → plan (date, time, place) → who’s free, as the PRD asks; booking straight from a companion’s profile still works. (5) Recipient consent window: 24 hours, then a full refund. (6) Gift validity: 3 months, refund if unused. (7) Package validity: 6 weeks (4) / 10 weeks (8); no package discount unless you set one. (8) New backend work: reviews, recipient consent, packages, gifts, payouts, per-city prices and content, analytics events, and Razorpay (needed for “authorised/captured”). The ID check stays at the first booking; companions without a verified ID show “New on prsnn”, never “ID verified”. Reply “approve all”, or name the screens to change.
